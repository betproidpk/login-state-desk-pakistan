Responsible Use

Betting is an adult activity that can cause financial loss. Treat entertainment spending as money that may be lost.

Set a money limit before you start. Set a time limit too. Do not borrow money to bet. Do not use rent, food, school, medical, debt, or emergency money. Do not chase losses by increasing the next stake.

Pause when betting starts to affect sleep, mood, work, study, relationships, or household money. A break is a valid action.

Login access should never become a reason to ignore security. Do not share passwords or security codes to regain access faster. Do not install unknown software or allow remote control of your device.

If you feel unable to stay within your limits, stop the activity and seek support from a trusted person or an appropriate safer-gambling service. The goal is safety, not continuous access.
