Editorial Policy

We write for readers first. Each page must answer a clear question in plain language.

For login and security topics, we prefer current primary sources such as Google Search Central, Google Chrome Help, OWASP, CISA, and the live money-site owner page when we need to define the project’s own routing.

We separate verified facts from observations and recommendations. We do not copy competitor claims as facts. Claims such as “official dealer,” “guaranteed payout,” “instant recovery,” or fixed provider timing require direct evidence before they can appear.

We avoid fake authors, fabricated testimonials, invented credentials, and copied screenshots that could imply official status.

This publication has a strict cannibalisation boundary: it owns the credential-state and access-route decision framework. Full login troubleshooting remains on the BetPro-ID.PK login owner page.
